SMS Privacy Policy

Silver Pharmacy, 1890 Silver Cross Blvd Suite 120, New Lenox, IL 60451 · Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy covers the patient text messaging program of Silver Pharmacy, an independent community pharmacy in New Lenox, Illinois. This policy is hosted on behalf of Silver Pharmacy by Surewell, Inc., which administers the messaging program.

Information we collect

We collect your name, mobile phone number, and your consent to receive text messages. Consent is collected verbally in person at the pharmacy counter and recorded in your patient profile in our pharmacy management system.

How we use your information

We use your mobile number only to send text messages about your prescriptions, pharmacy services, and insurance or benefit changes that affect you, and to reply to messages you send us.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except for vendors and service providers who help us deliver the messages, and as required by law. Protected health information is handled under HIPAA.

Opting out

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help, or call the pharmacy.

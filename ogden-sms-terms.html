Program description

When you opt in, Ogden Pharmacy sends you text messages from (630) 870-1897 about your prescriptions, pharmacy services, and insurance or benefit changes that affect you, and replies to messages you send us. These terms are hosted on behalf of Ogden Pharmacy by Surewell, Inc., which administers the messaging program.

How you opt in

You opt in verbally in person at the pharmacy counter. A staff member asks whether you would like to receive text messages, explains these terms, and records your mobile number and consent in your patient profile. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Message frequency and cost

  • Message frequency varies.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opting out and help

  • Reply STOP at any time to cancel. You will receive one final message confirming you are opted out.
  • Reply HELP for help, or call the pharmacy at (630) 320-8600.

Privacy

See the SMS Privacy Policy. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

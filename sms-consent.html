Employee Text Message (SMS) Consent

Part of the Surewell, Inc. onboarding acknowledgment · Effective August 18, 2026

Surewell, Inc. and its affiliated pharmacies use text messages to reach their own staff and representatives with brief operational messages. This is the written consent staff acknowledge during onboarding, alongside the rest of their new-hire paperwork.

How consent is collected

During onboarding, each staff member is asked to provide a mobile number and to sign the acknowledgment below. Signing is the employee's written opt-in. Providing a mobile number and consenting to texts is optional and is never a condition of employment.

Acknowledgment (signed during onboarding):

"I agree to receive operational text messages from Surewell and the pharmacy I work with at the mobile number I provided. These messages include requests to upload employment or compliance documents through a secure private link, reminders about outstanding items, and related account notices."

  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.
  • I can opt out at any time by replying STOP, or reply HELP for help.
  • Consent to receive texts is optional and is not a condition of employment or of any purchase.
  • My mobile information and SMS consent will not be shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes (see the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions).

What we send, and what we never do

  • Messages are strictly operational (document requests, reminders, account notices) to our own workforce.
  • No marketing or promotional messages are sent under this consent.
  • Mobile opt-in data and SMS consent are never shared or sold to any third party or affiliate for any purpose.
